**Publisher**: AburMD Software  
**Product**: AburMD  
**Effective Date**: August 10, 2026

## Factual Data Handling Commitments

AburMD is designed to operate 100% locally and offline on your computer.

### 1. Zero Telemetry & Tracking
AburMD does **not** collect, store, or transmit:
- Document contents or file names
- User activity, usage statistics, or telemetry
- Unique installation or device identifiers
- Personal data or browsing history

### 2. Local Document Processing
All document reading, parsing, syntax highlighting, and editing occur entirely in-memory on your local device. No documents are uploaded to cloud servers or third-party APIs.

### 3. Network Access
AburMD operates completely offline. Network access is only initiated when you explicitly click an external web link (`http://` or `https://`), which opens the link in your system's default web browser.

### 4. Contact & Support
If you reach out to support, any information you provide (such as bug reports) will be used solely to address your support request.
